## Framework

Each persona is defined by:
- **Need**: What they're trying to achieve
- **Context**: Their situation and constraints
- **Behaviour**: How they currently approach the problem
- **Pain points**: What frustrates them
- **Success criteria**: What "good" looks like for them
- **Priority**: How important this persona is to your business (P1/P2/P3)

## Personas

<!-- SETUP: Define 4-6 personas. Start with your most important users.

Example:

### The Efficiency Seeker (P1)
**Need:** Get the task done as fast as possible with minimum friction
**Context:** Uses the product daily as part of their workflow. Time-poor, technically competent.
**Behaviour:** Skips onboarding, goes straight to core features. Uses keyboard shortcuts. Gets frustrated by unnecessary steps.
**Pain points:**
- Extra clicks or confirmation dialogs
- Features that require reading documentation
- Slow load times
**Success criteria:** Task completed in under 2 minutes

### The Cautious Evaluator (P1)
**Need:** Feel confident they're making the right choice before committing
**Context:** Comparing multiple options. Risk-averse. Often a decision-maker spending company money.
**Behaviour:** Reads reviews, checks pricing carefully, wants to see social proof. Asks "what if?" questions.
**Pain points:**
- Hidden pricing or unclear terms
- No way to try before buying
- Lack of case studies or proof points
**Success criteria:** Feels informed and confident before committing

### The Reluctant User (P2)
**Need:** Complete a required task with minimum effort
**Context:** Doesn't want to use the product — was told to by their manager. Low motivation.
**Behaviour:** Does the bare minimum. Won't explore features. Abandons if confused.
**Pain points:**
- Anything that feels like unnecessary work
- Complex interfaces with too many options
- Lack of clear "what do I do next?" guidance
**Success criteria:** Finished the required task without frustration
