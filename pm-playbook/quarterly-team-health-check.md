# Quarterly Team Health Check

> **Owner:** {Your Name}
> **Frequency:** Once per quarter, completed before quarterly reviews
> **Audience:** All product squads (whole team: PM, EM, Designer, Engineers)
> **First run:** Q1 2026 — complete by Friday 4 April 2026

---

## Purpose

Review how your squad has been operating over the past quarter and identify specific actions to improve team effectiveness. This is not a performance review — it's a team-level reflection that surfaces what's working, what's not, and where to focus next quarter.

The health check gives us:
- A way for teams to self-diagnose and own their improvement
- Data points that help the leadership team spot systemic issues across the org and provide support
- A way for leadership to understand what challenges the team is facing and how they are planning to tackle them

---

## How it works

### Step 1: Individual scoring (async, ~15 mins per person)

Each team member independently scores every statement in the health check questionnaire below on a **1–5 scale**:

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | Strongly disagree — this is a significant problem |
| 2 | Disagree — we're struggling here |
| 3 | Neutral — it's okay but could be better |
| 4 | Agree — we're doing well |
| 5 | Strongly agree — this is a real strength |

For each statement, optionally add a short comment explaining your score or suggesting what could change. Comments are especially valuable on low scores (1–2) and high scores (4–5).

**Responses are anonymous.** This is important — honest scoring requires psychological safety.

#### Setting up the form

The PM is responsible for creating an anonymous form for their squad. Use Google Forms:

1. Create a new Google Form titled **"[Squad Name] — Q[X] 2026 Health Check"**
2. Add a **brief intro** at the top: _"Score each statement 1–5 (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree). Add a comment if you want to explain your score or suggest what could change. Responses are anonymous."_
3. For each statement in the three sections below, add a question with:
   - The statement text as the question title
   - A **linear scale** (1–5) as the answer type
   - A **short text** follow-up field: _"Why do you think this, and what could we do differently?"_ (set to optional)
4. Use **section headers** in the form to separate the three sections (Delivering impact, Process and best practice, Team health and satisfaction)
5. Under Settings, make sure **"Collect email addresses"** is turned off and **"Limit to 1 response"** is turned off (so that anonymity is preserved — don't require sign-in)
6. Share the form link in your squad Slack channel and give the team at least one full week to respond

Once responses are in, use the Google Forms summary view to pull out average scores per question. Copy these into a simple table for the team discussion.

### Step 2: Team discussion (in person or sync, ~45–60 mins)

The PM compiles the results and shares the **average score per statement and per section** with the team. Then run through and discuss the results together:

- **Acknowledge the highs as well as the areas for improvement.** Acknowledge what's working — these are strengths to protect and where things are difficult.
- **A healthy team is able to be critical and honest with itself and is looking for ways to improve.** The sign of an effective meeting is to have the hard conversations and to be honest about what hasnt been working and how you can work together to improve them.
- **Don't try to fix everything.** The goal is to identify the 1–3 most impactful actions the team can take next quarter.
- **Review how you're trending vs previous quarters.** Look at how scores in different categories are trending over time - are there areas that are improving following intervention or areas that are getting more difficult

### Step 3: Document and share

After the discussion, the PM documents:

1. **Section averages** (three numbers — one per section)
2. **Overall team average** (single number)
3. **Top 1–3 actions** the team has agreed to focus on next quarter

Share this summary with your manager ahead of the quarterly review. Keep it brief — a few lines per action is fine.

## Tips for PMs running this

- **Set up the form in advance** and give the team at least a full week to respond. Don't spring it on people in a meeting.
- **Keep the discussion forward-looking.** The scores tell you where you are; the conversation should focus on what to change.
- **Limit actions to 3 maximum.** More than three and nothing gets done. Pick the ones with the highest leverage.
- **Low scores are valuable, not alarming.** A team that scores itself honestly at 2.5 and takes action is healthier than a team that scores 4.5 and ignores problems.
- **Track actions quarter over quarter.** In the next health check, revisit whether the actions you set actually happened and whether they helped. This is how the process compounds.
- **Comments matter more than numbers.** The scores identify where to look; the comments tell you what's actually going on.

---

## What will we do with the results

- Review section and overall averages across all squads to identify common themes
- Use the results in quarterly reviews to discuss team effectiveness alongside delivery
- Spot systemic issues (e.g. if multiple teams score low on autonomy or cross-team collaboration) and escalate where needed
- Track trends over time — the goal is to see scores improve as teams take action
