# Manager Guide — Onboarding a New PM

A checklist for managers to run when onboarding a new PM into a team using this workspace. Work through these roughly in order — most of the "before Day 1" items are easy to miss when you're busy.

---

## Before Day 1

- [ ] **Confirm access requests are filed with IT** — Anthropic account, GitHub org, Granola. Don't assume HR has done this. Chase if needed.
- [ ] **Assign a peer buddy** — pick a PM who overlaps with their space or can answer day-to-day questions. Tell both people before the new PM starts.
- [ ] **Add them to Slack channels** — priority channels on Day 1: product-managers, product-launches, reviews, red-alerts, amber-alerts, plus any team-specific channels.
- [ ] **Block time for the Week 1 stakeholder intros** — exec calendars fill up fast. Book these before the PM starts, not after.
- [ ] **Send a welcome message** — brief Slack intro to the team before they join. Sets a good tone and means people recognise them on Day 1.

---

## Week 1: Setting them up for success

### Goal alignment (do on Day 1 or 2)

- [ ] **Have a goal-setting conversation** — don't leave this to drift. In the first 1:1, be explicit: what does good look like in 90 days? What's the one focus area they should go deep on first? Write it down.
- [ ] **Walk them through the roadmap** — your team's current quarter plan, what's in flight, what's coming, and how their role connects to the overall strategy. Include the squad's RAG status and any known risks.
- [ ] **Share the opportunity tree for their space** — if one exists, don't just point them at it. Walk through it together. Explain what's been validated, what's assumed, and where the biggest gaps are. Make clear it's a starting point to pressure-test, not a finished artefact.
- [ ] **Clarify their goal clearly** — outcome-oriented, measurable. "Increase {leading metric} from X to Y" not "own the {squad} squad." If Q goals aren't set yet, agree the first thing they should set goals on.

### Structure and ways of working

- [ ] **Set up the weekly 1:1** — recurring, in the calendar before Day 1. 45 mins recommended. Make it clear they can and should book ad-hoc time between sessions — don't leave them waiting a week to unblock a question.
- [ ] **Add them to team rituals** — PM sync, weekly trading meeting, their squad ceremonies, mid-Q check-ins. Check they're actually in the invites, not just told they exist.
- [ ] **Introduce AI-first ways of working** — explain that Claude Code is how this team operates, not an optional extra. Point them at Step 1 of their onboarding plan and offer to sit with them while they set it up if needed. Explain the quality bar: fast output is good, unreviewed output circulated to stakeholders is not.

### Context and business understanding

- [ ] **Walk them through the business context of their squad** — where does the team sit in the funnel? What upstream and downstream teams depend on their work? How does their squad's success metric connect to the P&L?
- [ ] **Book an operational immersion session** — whatever the equivalent is for your business (customer support shadowing, sales ride-along, warehouse walk-through, live-ops observation). Do it in Week 1 before it gets deprioritised.
- [ ] **Introduce them to their EM** — this should happen on Day 1 or 2. A PM-EM relationship takes time to build; don't let it be week 3 before they've had a proper handover conversation.

---

## Ongoing

- [ ] **First 1:1 agenda** — use `/meeting-prep` before every 1:1 from week 1 onwards. Start with: focus area agreed? Blockers? What have they read? What questions do they have?
- [ ] **Two-week check-in on tool adoption** — has Claude Code stuck? Are they using `/meeting-prep` and `/todo`? If not, unblock it in the next 1:1.
- [ ] **Review their first doc** — when they produce their first experiment or discovery doc, review it yourself before it goes anywhere. Use it as a teaching moment on evidence standards, hypothesis quality, and structure.
- [ ] **Use the onboarding milestones** — each PM onboarding plan has explicit milestones (end of Week 1, Week 2, etc.). Check against these at each 1:1 in the first 4 weeks. If they're behind, name it and agree what to cut or accelerate.
- [ ] **Operational immersion visits** — make sure these happen. They're easy to keep postponing. If the role involves customer-facing operations, the first immersion visit should be in Week 2 at the latest.

---

## What to make explicit

These are things that often get assumed but need to be spelled out:

- **Focus area agreed early** — if they don't have a clear answer to "what are you going deep on first?" by end of Week 1, that's a problem. The onboarding plan should force this but check it happened.
- **Evidence standards from Day 1** — PMs are expected to ground claims in data or research, not intuition. Set this expectation in the first 1:1. Point them at the evidence standards in the pm-playbook and CLAUDE.md.
- **Quality over speed on outputs** — Claude Code makes drafting fast. The risk is PMs circulate half-formed AI output and undermine trust. Be direct about this early.
- **Understand the full business model** — if your business has multiple sides (buyers/sellers, consumers/enterprise), make sure they understand how their work affects both sides. Many PMs arrive focused on one side only.
- **Peer buddy relationship** — check in on whether this is actually working. A buddy who doesn't have time isn't a buddy.
