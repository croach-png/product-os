# Discovery planning template

**Objective:** *A statement of what outcome you are hoping to achieve as a result of the discovery work.* EG. Understand user onboarding patterns and barriers in order to increase activation rate and paid conversion.

**Key questions:** *(these are the key questions that are critical to answer in order to achieve your discovery outcome)*

1. What are users needs, painpoints and desires?
2. How and where do users discover and evaluate tools like ours?
3. How do users decide which plan to choose and what drives their upgrade decision?

**Outputs:** *(what deliverables will you produce following this work)*

1. Detailed documented learnings
2. Key insights summary
3. Prioritised list of opportunities backed by insights
4. Next steps *(eg how do you move forwards to deliver value)*
