### ***Outline***

*Each product team or squad must complete this template in preparation for their quarterly roadmap review. Its purpose is to help us assess proposed plans for the coming quarter — including expected impact, key risks, and trade-offs — as well as to review progress made in the previous quarter.*

*Please:*

* *Please work within the template provided and keep the document concise **(ideally two pages)**.*

* *Complete and share the document **at least 48 hours before the team meeting**.*

* *Remove any text in **italics** before submission.*

* *Ensure the document is **open for comments**.*

* *All attendees should review and comment in advance.*

* *If success metrics are unclear, please resolve them ahead of the session.*

*The accompanying meeting is intended as a **two-way conversation**, giving the team the opportunity to discuss priorities, raise risks, and seek alignment on trade-offs.*

***Notes***

* *Keep numbers front and centre; use narrative to explain why.*

* *Avoid filler text — focus on alignment and decision-making.*
