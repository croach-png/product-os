# Analyse and Learn

**Objectives**

1. Experiment is swiftly analysed and concluded
2. Whole team has understood and extracted key learnings
3. Next steps are clear and communicated
4. Productionisation is planned and executed efficiently

| **Stage** | **Objective(s)** | **Lead & Involved** | **Timeline** | **Output(s)** | **Actions** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Post-experiment analysis** | Team is clear on the key insights on process and performance | Analytics - Lead, PM - support | Within 72 hours of the experiment ending. | 1. Experiment analysis planning document 2. Experiment analysis doc 3. Post-analysis meeting | 1. PM drafts key questions for analysis 2. PM & Analyst decide if experiment is to be turned off based on headline results (on completion). 3. PM reviews eval results from Logfire (or equivalent) alongside quantitative metrics — flag any quality or accuracy regressions. 4. Analyst shares the post-experiment analysis doc with the team before meeting (72hrs of completion). 5. BI & PM run post-experiment meeting so everyone is clear on learnings & next steps. |
| **2. Decision stage** | Decide on next steps and communicate | PM, with support from BI analyst, EM and designer. | Within 24 hours of post analysis meeting. | 1. Post-experiment document explaining what we learned and are doing next 2. Company wide email | 1. Update the post experiment doc and share with the product team 2. Update the Notion project page 3. Send wider message explaining the experiment's performance |
| **3. Productionisation scoping** | Understand productionisation requirements | EM & PM - Lead, Product team (dev, design, marketing) support. | Within 48 hours of post-analysis. | 1. Productionisation scoping meeting 2. Productionisation doc | 1. Workshop to collate key information to fill in the productionisation doc. 2. PM to share doc with the wider team 3. PM to prioritise work based on impact |
| **4. Productionisation delivery comms** | Stakeholders aligned with the productionisation plan. | Product Manager | — | Company wide email / slack update | 1. PM to share productionisation delivery doc and manage any comms during the build 2. Once the experience is launched, PM to send company-wide email. |
