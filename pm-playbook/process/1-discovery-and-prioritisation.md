# Discovery & Prioritisation

**Objectives:**

1. To discover and/or understand the most impactful opportunity we want to invest in unlocking

**Steps**:

1. Discovery Objective & Plan
2. Discovery activities
3. Prioritisation

| **Stage** | **Objective(s)** | **Lead & Involved** | **Output(s)** | **Actions** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Discovery Objective & Plan** | Team is clear on the desired outcome and has a discovery plan that maps how to get there | Product- Lead, Product Trio- Support | • Discovery plan | 1. Product to discuss and align with leadership 2. Product to communicate and align with the team 3. Product to document the discovery objective and plan ([using template](../templates/discovery-planning-template.md)) 4. Product to clearly define discovery activities, ownership & timelines |
| **2. Discovery activities** | Conduct discovery activities outlined in plan and shape opportunity tree / key findings | Product & Design- Lead, Team- Support | • Opportunity summary table • Key insights • Opportunity tree | 1. Undertake quant discovery to identify opportunities 2. Undertake qual discovery to understand why you're seeing that behaviour (interviews, surveys, customer feedback, user recordings) 3. Affinity mapping and/or user journey mapping to identify key problem hypothesis 4. Collate and prio opportunities and evidence in a [summary table](../templates/opportunity-summary-template.md) 5. Add opportunities to backlog & opportunity tree |
| **3. Prioritisation** | Prioritise and sequence the opportunities discovered | Product- Lead, Product Trio- Support | Stack rank of opportunities | 1. Product to evaluate each potential opportunity based on rough ICE (or IC at this stage) 2. Discuss and align with the team |
