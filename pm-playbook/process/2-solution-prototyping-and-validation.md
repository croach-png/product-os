# Solution Prototyping and Validation

**Objectives:**

1. To generate solution ideas that address the prioritised problem
2. Prototype and validate potential solutions

**Steps**:

1. Workshop planning
2. Idea generation
3. Idea prioritisation

| **Stage** | **Objective(s)** | **Lead & Involved** | **Output(s)** | **Actions** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Plan ideation workshop** | Workshop is planned to meet stated outcomes | PM & Designer - Lead | • Agenda and miro board for the workshop | 1. Clarify the hypothesis and definition of success. 2. Plan the workshop agenda including: what a successful outcome looks like, what ideation methods will be used, what evidence can you share related to the hypothesis |
| **2. Idea generation** | Generate and capture ideas to address the problem | Design - Lead, Team (and xfn) - Ideate | • HMW(s) to solve the problem or part of the problem • Brainstorm & ideate on potential solutions • Align on key concepts | 1. Share evidence related to the hypothesis while the team note their HMWs 2. Group HMWs and brainstorm possible solutions. Use AI to help generate ideas (should get 100's) 3. Team to rough sketch individual solutions, use techniques such as Crazy 8s etc. 4. Team to prototype solutions using AI prototyping tool 5. Team to identify the key concepts from the workshop to include in design |
| **3. Prototype key concepts** | Create prototypes and test | Product Trio - Lead | • 3+ solution directions prototyped for validation | 1. Create (or edit existing) prototypes around key concepts — ideally at least 3 for bigger bets to prepare them for validation 2. Use AI to get feedback on prototypes against hypothesis and to identify assumptions not identified 3. Share prototypes with ideation team 4. Prototypes are shared with product and design leaders to align on direction |
| **4. Idea validation** | Validate & agree solution | Design - Lead, Product - Support | • Feedback on solution directions from user testing • Prototype iterated on | 1. Prototypes are user tested / validated against initial hypothesis and assumptions 2. Feedback is collated and prototypes are iterated on based on feedback — another round of user testing if helpful 3. Final concept agreed **Note: Steps 1-4 should be completed in maximum 2 weeks** |
