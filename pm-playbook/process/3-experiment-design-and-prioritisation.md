# Experiment Design & Prioritisation

**Objectives:**

- To clearly outline the experiment hypothesis, method and key metrics.
- Ensure solution meets these expectations.
- Use ROTI score to prioritise the most impactful experiments.

**Steps**:

1. Write up experiment / project
2. Calculate experiment metrics
3. High level ROTI estimate
4. Prioritise experiments
5. Identify tracking needs

| **Stage** | **Objective** | **Lead & Involved** | **Output(s)** | **Actions** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Write up experiment** | Clear hypothesis and execution plan | Product Manager | New experiment template doc | 1. [Write up new experiment doc (using template)](../templates/experiment-template.md). |
| **2. Calculate experiment metrics** | Confirm impact & runtime are pragmatic | PM - Lead, Analytics - Review | Experiment method, uplift, run time and revenue impact calculated | 1. Run pre-test analysis. 2. Determine estimated experiment run time. 3. Determine success metrics and expected revenue uplift. 4. Agree with team members |
| **3. High level ROTI estimate & highlight assumptions** | Prioritise using ROTI scoring | PM, EM, Design and BI | Completed first draft ROTI scoring template including high level estimates | 1. Discussion with team leads to create high-level estimates of design, build, test. 2. Calculate ROTI — is the experiment worth the effort? Does scope need limiting? 3. Identify assumptions and any tests needed to derisk. |
| **4. Prioritise experiments** | Prioritise experiments | Product Manager | Updated experiment backlog / roadmap | 1. Prioritise experiments based on ROTI scores and add to backlog |
| **5. Identify tracking needs** | Confirm tracking needs | PM (Lead), Analyst, EM and Designer | Tracking spreadsheet completed | 1. Complete [tracking template](../templates/event-tracking-template.md) — identify learnings and prioritise |
