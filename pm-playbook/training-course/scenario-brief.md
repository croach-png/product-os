# Scenario Brief: Onboarding Drop-off

*This is a fabricated scenario for training purposes. Numbers and quotes are illustrative, not real company data.*

---

## Your role

You're a new PM on the **Onboarding squad**. You report to Alex (Product Director). Your squad's core metric is **activated users and paid conversions**.

## Your team

| Role | Name | Focus |
|------|------|-------|
| Product Manager | You | Onboarding flow — discovery through delivery |
| Tech Lead | Sam | Frontend and backend for the onboarding flow |
| Designer | Priya | User experience across sign-up and onboarding |
| Data Analyst | Jordan | Funnel analytics, experiment sizing and analysis |

You also work closely with:
- The **Growth** squad (acquisition and sign-up conversion, upstream of you)
- The **Engagement** squad (feature adoption and retention, downstream of you)
- **Customer Success** (supporting users through onboarding and beyond)

## The business context

The company is a B2B SaaS platform for project management. Teams sign up for a 14-day free trial, onboard their team, and convert to paid plans. Revenue comes from per-seat subscriptions ($49/user/month for Pro, $89/user/month for Enterprise).

**Key funnel:** Sign-up → Profile Setup → Invite Teammates → First Project → Activated → Paid

- **3,200 sign-ups per week** (from organic, paid acquisition, and referrals)
- **22% activation rate** (sign-up to activated)
- **68% paid conversion** among activated users
- **$49 ARPU** (blended, per user per month)

## The problem

**78% of free trial sign-ups never complete onboarding.** The biggest single drop-off is at the "invite teammates" step:

| Step | Weekly volume | Step completion | Cumulative |
|------|-------------|-----------------|------------|
| Sign-up | 3,200 | — | 100% |
| Profile setup | 2,880 | 90% | 90% |
| Invite teammates | 1,094 | 38% | 34% |
| First project | 877 | 80% | 27% |
| Activated | 704 | 80% | 22% |
| Paid (within 30 days) | 479 | 68% | 15% |

**62% of users who complete profile setup never send a team invite.** But once they invite one person, 85% go on to complete onboarding. The invite step is the critical activation bottleneck.

## Key pain points (from research)

- Users don't see the value of inviting others at this stage — "I wanted to try it myself first"
- The invite form feels like a commitment — entering colleagues' emails before seeing value
- Solo users don't understand why collaboration features are being pushed
- The invite step has no skip option — users feel stuck and abandon
- No progress indicator — users don't know how many steps remain

## What's been tried

- Added a "skip for now" option (6 months ago) — improved flow completion by 12% but reduced team invites by 40%
- Sent reminder emails at day 3 and day 7 — 8% open rate, 2% conversion
- Shortened onboarding from 6 steps to 4 — improved completion by 15% but the invite step drop-off remained

## Your mission

Alex has asked you to own the discovery and experiment design for improving the invite-step completion rate. The goal is to move activation rate from 22% to 30% within 2 quarters.
