## Framework

Each persona is defined by need, context, behaviour, pain points, and success criteria.

---

## Personas

### The Upgrader (P1 — Seller)
**Need:** Sell their current bike quickly and at a fair price to fund an upgrade
**Context:** Enthusiast cyclist, owns a decent bike (£500-£2,000), wants to move up. Knows the market value roughly. Has sold on eBay or Facebook before.
**Behaviour:** Compares Velo's suggested price to eBay sold listings. Will abandon if the process takes too long or the price feels low. Wants to list in under 5 minutes.
**Pain points:**
- Condition checklist feels like overkill for a well-maintained bike
- 3-day payment delay is frustrating — "I've already bought the new bike"
- Doesn't want to deal with tyre-kickers making lowball offers
**Success criteria:** Bike listed in under 5 minutes, sold within a week, paid promptly

### The First-Timer Buyer (P1 — Buyer)
**Need:** Buy a reliable used bike without getting ripped off
**Context:** Getting into cycling or commuting. Doesn't know much about bikes. Budget £150-£500. Anxious about buying something that breaks.
**Behaviour:** Reads condition reports carefully. Compares multiple listings. Asks support about sizing. Wants reassurance (reviews, guarantees, return policy).
**Pain points:**
- Doesn't know what size bike they need
- Can't tell from photos if a bike is actually in good condition
- Worried about buying online without test-riding
**Success criteria:** Feels confident the bike is good quality and the right size before purchasing

### The Bargain Hunter (P2 — Buyer)
**Need:** Get the best possible deal on a used bike
**Context:** Price-sensitive, patient, willing to wait for the right listing. Often browses daily. May use Klarna.
**Behaviour:** Sets up alerts for specific models. Makes offers below asking price. Compares across Velo, eBay, and Facebook Marketplace simultaneously.
**Pain points:**
- No easy way to track price drops on watched bikes
- Offer system feels slow — wants instant counter-offers
- Delivery fee on top of purchase price feels like a hidden cost
**Success criteria:** Paid less than market value, feels like they got a deal

### The Reluctant Seller (P2 — Seller)
**Need:** Get rid of a bike they no longer use with minimum effort
**Context:** Has a bike in the garage they haven't ridden in years. Doesn't know what it's worth. Not motivated enough to deal with strangers on Facebook.
**Behaviour:** Needs the process to be extremely simple. Will abandon at the first sign of complexity. Doesn't care about maximising price — just wants it gone.
**Pain points:**
- Doesn't know the brand/model/year — just "it's a blue mountain bike"
- Taking 6+ photos feels like too much work
- Condition checklist assumes knowledge they don't have
**Success criteria:** Listed in under 3 minutes with minimal decisions

### The Shop Partner (P3 — B2B)
**Need:** Shift used trade-in stock efficiently without cannibalising new bike sales
**Context:** Independent bike shop that takes trade-ins. Has 10-30 used bikes at any time. Currently sells via shop floor and eBay.
**Behaviour:** Wants bulk listing tools. Needs to maintain their shop brand. Cares about margin, not speed.
**Pain points:**
- Listing bikes one-by-one is too slow
- No way to link Velo listings back to their shop
- 8% seller fee is high when they're already on thin margins
**Success criteria:** All trade-in stock listed in under 30 minutes, sold within 2 weeks
