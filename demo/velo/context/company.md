## Company Background

**Founded:** 2021
**Headquarters:** Bristol, UK
**Stage:** Series A (£18M raised)
**Employees:** ~85
**Valuation:** ~£80M

Velo is an online marketplace for buying and selling used bicycles. Sellers list their bikes with photos and a condition report, and Velo handles valuation, payment, and logistics. Buyers browse verified listings with guaranteed condition ratings and get doorstep delivery.

## Company Mission

**Mission:** Make second-hand bikes the first choice for every rider.

**Values:**
- **Trust first** — Every transaction should feel safe for both sides
- **Evidence over instinct** — We test before we commit
- **Sustainability matters** — Every used bike sold is a new bike not manufactured

## Key Metrics

- **GMV:** £2.1M/month (target: £3.5M by end of Q2 2026)
- **Volume:** ~4,200 bikes sold/month
- **Take rate:** 12% average (seller fee 8%, buyer fee 4%)
- **Unit economics:** £52 average revenue per transaction, £31 contribution margin

---

## Market Position

### Business Model

Two-sided marketplace. Sellers list bikes for free; Velo charges an 8% seller fee on completed sales and a 4% buyer service fee. Premium listings (featured placement, professional photos) available for £15.

Revenue split: 70% transaction fees, 20% premium listings, 10% delivery margin.

### Key Competitors

- **Bike Exchange** (direct — established global bike marketplace, weaker in UK)
- **Facebook Marketplace** (indirect — free, no trust layer, high fraud risk)
- **eBay** (indirect — general marketplace, poor bike-specific experience)
- **Swapfiets** (adjacent — subscription bikes, expanding into resale)
