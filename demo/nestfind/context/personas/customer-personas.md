## Framework

Each persona is defined by need, context, behaviour, pain points, and success criteria.

---

## Personas

### The Accidental Landlord (P1 — Landlord)
**Need:** Rent out a property they didn't plan to be a landlord for — inherited, couldn't sell, relocated for work
**Context:** Has one property, no letting experience. Nervous about bad tenants and legal obligations. Probably got a quote from a high-street agent (14-18% fee) and was shocked.
**Behaviour:** Researches extensively before listing. Reads every review. Wants hand-holding through the process. Will abandon if anything feels risky or confusing.
**Pain points:**
- Doesn't know what rent to charge — terrified of pricing too high or too low
- Overwhelmed by legal requirements (gas safety, EPC, deposit protection)
- Anxious about problem tenants with no experience to judge applications
**Success criteria:** Property let within 3 weeks, tenant passes referencing, feels confident they've done everything legally

### The Portfolio Pro (P1 — Landlord)
**Need:** Manage 5-20 properties efficiently without paying agent fees
**Context:** Experienced landlord, previously used letting agents but frustrated by fees and poor service. Knows the market well. Wants control and transparency.
**Behaviour:** Lists quickly, prices accurately, responds to enquiries fast. Judges platforms on efficiency and cost. Will use multiple platforms simultaneously.
**Pain points:**
- No bulk tools — has to list properties one at a time
- Can't see portfolio-level analytics (yield, void periods, maintenance costs)
- Referencing is too slow — loses good tenants to faster agents
**Success criteria:** All properties listed in under 30 minutes, void periods under 2 weeks, total cost under £100/month

### The First-Time Renter (P1 — Tenant)
**Need:** Find their first rental property without getting scammed or overwhelmed
**Context:** Just graduated or starting first job. Budget £600-£900/month. Has never signed a lease. Doesn't know what referencing means. Worried about deposits.
**Behaviour:** Browses daily, saves lots of listings, hesitates to apply. Asks friends for advice. Needs reassurance at every step.
**Pain points:**
- Doesn't understand the application process or timeline
- Deposit (5 weeks' rent) is a huge financial barrier
- Can't tell from photos if a property is actually decent — wants to see it in person but viewings are hard to schedule
**Success criteria:** Understands every step before it happens, deposit is manageable, moves in without surprises

### The Relocator (P2 — Tenant)
**Need:** Find a rental quickly from a different city — can't easily visit in person
**Context:** Moving for a new job, has 2-4 weeks to find somewhere. Currently 100+ miles away. Willing to pay more for certainty and speed.
**Behaviour:** Applies to multiple properties simultaneously. Values virtual tours highly. Will pay for premium features if they save time. Makes decisions fast.
**Pain points:**
- Can't attend in-person viewings — needs virtual alternatives
- Referencing takes too long when they need to move in 2 weeks
- Competing against local applicants who can view and decide faster
**Success criteria:** Tenancy secured within 10 days, moved in within 3 weeks, no surprises on move-in day

### The Letting Agent (P3 — B2B)
**Need:** Use NestFind as a channel to fill vacancies for their managed portfolio
**Context:** Small independent agency, 50-150 properties under management. Currently lists on Rightmove/Zoopla at £300+/month. Looking for cheaper alternatives.
**Pain points:**
- Listing one-by-one is too slow for their volume
- No API for their property management software
- Brand visibility — their agency name gets lost on the platform
**Success criteria:** All vacancies listed via bulk upload, agency branding visible, cost under £150/month
