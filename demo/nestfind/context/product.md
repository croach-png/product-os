## User Journeys

### Landlord Journey

1. **Create listing** — Landlord enters property address (auto-fills council tax band, EPC rating). Uploads photos or books a NestFind photographer. AI generates a listing description from photos and details. ~8 minutes.
2. **Set terms** — Landlord sets rent, deposit, move-in date, tenancy length, and pet/smoking policy. NestFind suggests rent based on comparable local listings.
3. **Listing goes live** — Appears in search. Landlord receives applications with tenant profiles, employment details, and affordability scores.
4. **Select tenant** — Landlord reviews applications, arranges viewings (in-person or virtual), and selects a tenant. NestFind handles referencing.
5. **Move-in** — NestFind generates the tenancy agreement, protects the deposit, and coordinates key handover.

**Key pain points:**
- Photo upload is the biggest friction point — 48% of landlords abandon during photo/description step
- Landlords don't trust the AI rent suggestion if it's lower than they expected
- Referencing takes 5 days on average — landlords lose impatient tenants to faster competitors

### Tenant Journey

1. **Search** — Tenants find NestFind via Google, social, or word of mouth. Search by area, budget, bedrooms, and filters (pets, parking, garden).
2. **Browse & shortlist** — View photos, virtual tours, floor plans, EPC rating, local area info. Save to shortlist.
3. **Apply** — Submit application with employment details, income, and references. One application works for multiple properties.
4. **Referencing & offer** — NestFind runs referencing (employment, credit, previous landlord). Landlord makes an offer.
5. **Sign & move in** — E-sign tenancy agreement, pay deposit and first month's rent, receive move-in checklist.

**Key pain points:**
- Tenants apply to 6+ properties on average — most get no response from landlords
- Referencing feels like a black box — tenants don't know what's happening or how long it'll take
- Deposit is the #1 financial barrier — 35% of tenants say it delayed their move
