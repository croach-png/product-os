## Company Background

**Founded:** 2020
**Headquarters:** Manchester, UK
**Stage:** Series B (£45M raised)
**Employees:** ~180

NestFind is an online rental marketplace that connects landlords with verified tenants. Landlords list properties for free with AI-assisted descriptions and virtual tours. Tenants browse, apply, and sign leases entirely online. NestFind handles referencing, deposit protection, and move-in coordination.

## Company Mission

**Mission:** Make renting as simple and transparent as booking a hotel.

**Values:**
- **Tenant-first** — A great tenant experience attracts great landlords
- **Transparency** — No hidden fees, clear timelines, honest listings

## Key Metrics

- **GMV:** £12M/month in annual rent value listed (target: £20M by end of Q2 2026)
- **Volume:** ~3,200 tenancies started/month
- **Take rate:** 8% of first month's rent (tenant fee) + £49/month landlord subscription
- **Unit economics:** £135 average revenue per tenancy, £62 contribution margin

## Market Position

### Business Model

Two-sided fee model. Tenants pay a service fee (8% of first month's rent) covering referencing, deposit protection, and move-in coordination. Landlords pay a monthly subscription (£49/month for standard, £99/month for premium with virtual tours and priority placement).

Revenue split: 55% tenant fees, 35% landlord subscriptions, 10% ancillary (insurance referrals, inventory services).

### Key Competitors

- **OpenRent** (direct — self-serve landlord platform, lower fees, less tenant tooling)
- **SpareRoom** (adjacent — room-share focused, expanding into whole properties)
- **Rightmove/Zoopla** (incumbent — dominant listings, moving into transactions)
- **Goodlord** (B2B — letting agent software, not direct-to-landlord)
